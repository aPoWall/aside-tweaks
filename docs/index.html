what happened

01 · ⌘D

keep the page, keep the tab.

as in Arc: the page joins the end of the bookmarks bar, the rows above it keep their places, and the tab stays open and selected. press ⌘D again on that page to take the row out.

02 · ⇧⌘K

search the whole desk.

tabs, history, bookmarks, obsidian notes, aside menu items, commands and configured agents share one field. ⌘K opens the same action panel on every row type, and ⌘1…⌘9 switch to the blocks the palette lists by number. declared exception to family rule 37: the browser keeps ⌘K, so the palette opens on ⇧⌘K and ⌘K stays the row action panel (README · family keys).

03 · ⌥⌘D

review before cleanup.

exact copies, related products, stale events and research sources stay in separate groups until you choose what to keep, bookmark or close. every batch writes a local receipt.

04 · live mark

a character in the header.

the panel and popup carry the light aside character: a panel frame, three row sticks, a red cursor. it lifts under the cursor, scatters and reassembles on click in 0.8 s, and the cursor steps to the next row. reduce motion keeps the step only.

unpacked extension · 4.22.0

install aside tweaks.

  1. clone the canonical repository.
    git clone https://github.com/aPoWall/aside-tweaks.git
  2. open chrome://extensions in aside, enable developer mode, press load unpacked and choose the repository folder.
  3. optional local desk bridge for obsidian notes, aside menus and agent launch points. it listens on loopback only.
    bridge/install.sh
curl -H 'X-Aside-Tweaks: desk' http://127.0.0.1:49321/health

update and rollback

pull the repository and press reload on the extension card. 4.22.0 keeps keymaps, bookmarks, review state, bridge config and theme settings, and switches ⌘D to the Arc contract once: closing the tab and moving it to the top are set to off and stay available in settings. rollback: check out 4.20, press reload; bookmark order and review storage stay intact.

your tabs stay in your browser.

review classification runs locally. the extension has no server, no telemetry and no account. receipts live in local extension storage.

optional model grouping sends only tab titles and hosts to the openrouter model you configure and stores the key locally. it proposes groups in a preview window and never applies them on its own.

the desk bridge answers only on 127.0.0.1 with a configured header. page content, cookies and form values stay outside its protocol.

shared n1 profile.

version 4.22.0 takes the shell of the apps design system: one header, one bottom line, one product mark. aim-app-shell.css, aim-app-mark.js and aim-app-marks.svg are vendored byte for byte and checked by sha-256, and the toolbar icon is rendered from the same mark. the voxel character stays on this page as an illustration.
